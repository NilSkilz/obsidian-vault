# Rope Film (idea, parked)

Logged 1 Nov 2026 (03:00, Rob couldn't sleep). Just noting it, no commitment.

## The idea

A short (~3 min) art film cut to one piece of music, slow and luminous, the "Nature of Daylight" (Max Richter) kind of register. Rope as the subject: leading someone into the woods, tying, suspending. Well shot, arty, not porny.

Rob's own read: needs more of a theme and an arc before it's a real project.

## Arc seeds (Jarvis, first pass, to argue with)

- **Trust as the spine.** Starts with the walk in: hand-holding, leading, nobody tied yet. The rope is the second act, not the first. Ends on the untie and aftercare, which is the bit nobody films and is the most honest part.
- **Light as the clock.** Shoot it across one golden hour or dawn: blue start, gold peak at the suspension, dusk on the release. The music and the light rise and fall together, so the arc is built in.
- **Rope as a thread through the woods.** Opens with a line of rope already laid through the trees, the pair follow it in. The tie is where the thread ends.
- **Mirror the trees.** Bark, roots, hanging branches cut against wraps, knots, a suspended body. Visual rhymes do the storytelling, no dialogue needed.

## Practical notes

- Pairs naturally with [[Location Scouting]] (woods with a solid limb, quiet, reachable with a kit). Luxulyan Valley is the obvious candidate: wooded, ruins, quieter than the coast.
- Tree suspension = Rob's in-person call on the limb, always.
- Music licensing matters if it's ever shown publicly (Richter etc. won't be free); a commissioned or royalty-free track avoids that.
- Faces optional; tight crops and silhouettes keep it arty and keep the people private.
