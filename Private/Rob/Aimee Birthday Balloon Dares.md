# Aimee's Birthday: Balloon Dares

*Idea captured 2 Oct 2026. Surprise planning, stays in Private/Rob.*

**Concept:** dares/forfeits written on cards, hidden inside balloons. Guests pop a balloon and do the dare. BDSM-flavoured, playful, not too sexual.

## The ten dares

1. Snog the birthday girl
2. Five spanks from Aimee, implement of her choosing
3. Be Aimee's furniture for 10 minutes (footstool, drinks table, her call)
4. Collar and lead for the next hour, Aimee holds the lead
5. Aimee writes one word on you in marker, worn visibly all night
6. Hands tied behind your back for your next drink, a guest of Aimee's choosing feeds it to you
7. Blindfold taste test: Aimee feeds you three things, guess all three or take a forfeit spank each
8. Address everyone as Sir or Madam for 30 minutes, every slip earns Aimee a free swat
9. Peg challenge: Aimee places five clothes pegs (clothing or skin, popper's choice), survive 10 minutes
10. Royal treatment: kneel and present Aimee's drink, then a 2-minute shoulder massage, redeemable whenever she snaps her fingers

## Mechanics

- Mix in a couple of blanks or "nominate someone else" cards for unpredictability.
- House rule: any dare can be swapped for popping another balloon (consent-clean exit, no vibe kill).
- Kit needed: balloons, card slips, marker, collar + lead, clothes pegs, blindfold, something tie-friendly.
