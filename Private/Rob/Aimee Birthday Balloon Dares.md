# Aimee's Birthday: Balloon Dares

*Idea captured 2 Oct 2026. Redone 4 Oct: Aimee is the SUB, dares point at her, not from her. Surprise planning, stays in Private/Rob.*

**Concept:** dares/forfeits written on cards, hidden inside balloons. Guests pop a balloon and get a dare that puts the birthday girl on the receiving end. BDSM-flavoured, playful, a couple of cards run warmer but nothing full-on.

## The ten dares (v2, sub Aimee)

1. **Snog the birthday girl.** The classic, still the ace of the deck.
2. **Spank delivery.** You pick the implement and the number (max five), Rob delivers while you count them out loud for her.
3. **Pet on parade.** Collar and lead go on Aimee; you walk her one lap of the room and introduce her to someone as "the birthday brat."
4. **Brand her.** Write one word on Aimee in marker (arm, shoulder, chest, your call within clothing lines). She wears it the rest of the night.
5. **Service with a smile.** Aimee kneels, fetches you a drink of your choosing, and presents it properly. She stays kneeling until you've taken the first sip.
6. **Blindfold taste test.** Blindfold Aimee and feed her three mystery things. Every wrong guess earns her a swat, delivered by Rob on your count.
7. **Yes Sir, yes Madam.** For the next 30 minutes Aimee addresses you (just you) as Sir or Madam. Every slip, you add a peg to her tally for dare 8's holder.
8. **Peg her.** Place five clothes pegs on Aimee, clothing or skin, her call on where. She keeps them on for 10 minutes.
9. **Body shot.** Salt, shot, lime, taken off the birthday girl. Her collarbone is traditional. *(warmer)*
10. **Private dance.** Aimee gives you a 60-second lap dance, music of your choosing. No hands, that's the rule for you, not her. *(warmer)*

## Mechanics

- **Impact stays Rob's.** Guests choose and count, Rob swings. Keeps the "nobody else tops Aimee" line from the scene plan intact, and honestly it's hotter: the guest commands, she takes it from you.
- **Aimee's exit:** she gets the same house rule as everyone, any dare can be traded for popping another balloon, plus her safeword ends anything dead. Brief her on the deck's *existence* and the trade rule before the first pop (not the contents), so the consent is real but the cards stay a surprise.
- **Pacing:** every card lands on her now, so she can't absorb a popping frenzy. Cap it at one dare active at a time, and mix in blanks and a couple of "the popper does a forfeit instead" reversal cards so she gets breathers and nobody can predict a pop.
