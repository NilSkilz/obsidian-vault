## Safety notes worth keeping

- **Cold is the real risk.** December + mud + wet skin = hypothermia inside about an hour. Dryrobe/blanket, flask, car with heater running, in-role warm-up breaks.
- **Hair dragging:** grip right at the root near the scalp, never take bodyweight by the hair, never wrench the neck back.
- **Walk the ground first** for glass, brambles, barbed wire, dog mess before any mud dragging.
- **Non-verbal safeword that works through a gag** (dropped object or taps), hard limits agreed in writing beforehand.
- **Location must be properly private** — not a public footpath. A dog walker seeing a "kidnap" ends the day in a police car; also risks outraging public decency. Worth cross-checking [[../../Context/Location Scouting|Location Scouting]] for somewhere private before planning dates.
- Full aftercare kit (change of clothes, towel, wipes, food, warmth) plus a next-day check-in given the likely drop after a degradation scene.
