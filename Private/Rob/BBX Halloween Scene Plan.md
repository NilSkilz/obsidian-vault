## Key safety calls
- **Air:** prime both the saline and lube lines fully before anything goes in. Air pumped into the vagina is the one serious risk in the AI section (air embolism). It's rare, but it's catastrophic. Lines must be bubble-free, and no plug or seal may be in place while fluid is pumping, so it can always escape.
- **Tube tip:** use a soft, smooth, rounded end, a catheter-tip syringe, or a toy with a channel. Never use a cut tube edge, and don't push it up against the cervix.
- **Lube:** make it up fresh on Saturday with boiled-and-cooled or distilled water, in a clean bottle. Made-up lube is a bacteria farm after a day or two. Avoid sugar, starch, cornflour or glycerine-heavy additives (thrush). Use the same whitener as last time if it went fine.
- **Warming:** stand the lube bottle in a water bath in the wax warmer and check it with a probe thermometer: 37 to 38°C, never over 40. **Empty and dry the warmer completely before any wax goes in**, because water in molten wax spits.
- **Saline rate:** last time the fast fill stung. Run it slower at the club. Let the IPA dry fully on her skin. Use warm saline if possible.
- **Sensory deprivation:** with a blindfold, ear defenders, a blanket and bondage, she can't hear a check-in, so she needs a **non-verbal stop signal** (dropping a held object, or a hand-squeeze code). Rob stays within arm's reach and in hand contact the whole time. Bring safety shears.
