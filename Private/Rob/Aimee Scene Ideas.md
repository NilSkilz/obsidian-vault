Rob's idea bank for scenes with Aimee. Private because half the value is surprise. Fully planned scenes graduate to their own `<Name> Scene Plan.md` (see [[wax-pour-scene-plan]]).

## Ideas (added 7 Nov 2026)

### What3Words
Take her to the woods, tie her up, take a pic. "Share" the photo plus the what3words on FetLife and show her. Mindfuck: the freak-out is thinking strangers now know exactly where she's tied.
- Jarvis note: make the post fake (a draft, a screenshot mockup, or a private/friends-only post you delete straight after). A real public pin on a bound woman is a genuine risk, and her face on Fet without a prior yes is a consent problem she can't take back. The scare works just as well when it's fake.
- Location scouting overlap: see `Context/Location Scouting.md` (Wenford Dries benchmark).

### FireRope
Suspension where the upline is on fire, with metal rope underneath taking the weight.
- Jarvis note: the hardest one to do safely. Steel conducts heat to the hardware and the load path. Burning jute drops embers and molten bits downward onto her, and you can't do a fast cut-down through steel. Possible directions: fire on a decorative line that isn't the upline and is offset from her, or fire-poi-style wicks on a separate line. Needs a fire-safety spotter, a fire blanket and an extinguisher at minimum. Plan properly before any test.

### Stake Burning
Burning at the stake. Needs planning.
- Jarvis note: the look, not the heat. Options: an LED/silk flame effect, fog plus orange uplighting, a ring of real flame kept well clear on a non-combustible base. Quick-release ties to the stake. Pairs with the scaffold rig as the stake.

### Whiplash
Scaffolding rigged in a whipping-post position, whip on her back.
- Jarvis note: easiest of the four with kit you already have ([[Scaffold Rig]]). Check the frame for sideways load when she pulls or leans, and keep the whip on the meaty upper back and shoulders, away from the spine and kidneys.
