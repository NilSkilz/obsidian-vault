## Ideas (added 7 Nov 2026)

### What3Words
Take her to the woods, tie her up, take a pic. "Share" the photo plus the what3words on FetLife and show her. Mindfuck: the freak-out is thinking strangers now know exactly where she's tied.
- Jarvis note: make the post fake (a draft, a screenshot mockup, or a private/friends-only post you delete straight after). A real public pin on a bound woman is a genuine risk, and her face on Fet without a prior yes is a consent problem she can't take back. The scare works just as well when it's fake.
- Location scouting overlap: see `Context/Location Scouting.md` (Wenford Dries benchmark).

### FireRope
Suspension where the upline is on fire, with metal rope underneath taking the weight.
- Jarvis note: the hardest one to do safely. Steel conducts heat to the hardware and the load path. Burning jute drops embers and molten bits downward onto her, and you can't do a fast cut-down through steel. Possible directions: fire on a decorative line that isn't the upline and is offset from her, or fire-poi-style wicks on a separate line. Needs a fire-safety spotter, a fire blanket and an extinguisher at minimum. Plan properly before any test.

### Stake Burning
Burning at the stake. Needs planning.
- Jarvis note: the look, not the heat. Options: an LED/silk flame effect, fog plus orange uplighting, a ring of real flame kept well clear on a non-combustible base. Quick-release ties to the stake. Pairs with the scaffold rig as the stake.

### Whiplash
Scaffolding rigged in a whipping-post position, whip on her back.
- Jarvis note: easiest of the four with kit you already have ([[Scaffold Rig]]). Check the frame for sideways load when she pulls or leans, and keep the whip on the meaty upper back and shoulders, away from the spine and kidneys.

## Witch-trial batch (added 7 Nov 2026)
Aimee loves the "witch" genre (burning, whipping, trial). She does NOT like water, so no ducking stool or dunking ordeals. Most of these chain together into one long trial scene: accusation, search, ordeal, confession, sentence (Whiplash / Stake Burning).

### The Pricking
Historic witch-prickers searched for the "devil's mark", a spot that felt no pain. Blindfold her, run a Wartenberg pinwheel and pinches over her, and she has to say each time whether she felt it. Every wrong answer counts as proof. The interrogation is the scene.
- Jarvis note: pinwheel is all you need. Real needles only if you've both done play piercing properly (sterile, single-use, sharps bin).

### Marking the Devil's Marks
Strip-search "inspection", circle every mole and freckle in marker as a witch's mark, then each mark earns a stroke.
- Jarvis note: body-safe skin marker, not a Sharpie. Feeds straight into Whiplash.

### Ordeal by Hot Iron (mindfuck brand)
Heat a poker or a brand where she can see it and hear it hiss. Blindfold her, swap it for an identical cold one, or for ice. Cold steel on skin straight after that build-up reads as a burn for a split second.
- Jarvis note: the swap must be foolproof. Hot iron goes straight into a bucket of sand, out of reach, before the cold one comes out. Never both in hand at once.

### The Witch's Cradle
A real historic torture: the accused was put in a sack and hung up to swing and spin until she was disoriented. Suspension version: hood or blindfold, rope cocoon or hammock, slow spin, no sense of up.
- Jarvis note: spinning plus suspension means nausea and fainting. Keep it short, keep her head higher than her hips, use a swivel on the hard point, and keep shears to hand.

### Scold's Bridle
An iron head-cage gag, the historic punishment for women who talked back. Buy one or make one from steel strip. The scene works as a punishment within the trial for "insolence".
- Jarvis note: anything in the mouth means a non-verbal safe signal (dropped bell or ball). Smooth and rounded tongue plate, no sharp edges.

### The Circle (ritual)
She's tied spread in a ring of candles or salt. Wax play as the "summoning", then you play the inquisitor who breaks the circle.
- Jarvis note: low-temp play candles, nothing perfumed or beeswax. Keep the ring far enough out that hair and rope can't reach a flame. Links to [[wax-pour-scene-plan]].

### Signing the Confession
Write out a confession in old-style script. She refuses to sign, and each refusal earns another round (thumbscrew-style finger clamps, a cane). The scene ends when she signs. Keep the signed one; it's a great souvenir.

### Stocks in the Square
Scaffold-built stocks (head and wrists), with her on display for the village. Pair it with the What3Words fake-post fear, or with dirty talk about the "crowd". Flogging or caning while she's stuck there.
- Jarvis note: wrist and neck holes need to be big and padded, never tight on the throat. Hinge with a quick-release pin. Fits [[Scaffold Rig]].

### Nettles (folk punishment)
Nettle switching was a real folk punishment. Stings for hours, very witchy.
- Jarvis note: seasonal. November nettles are dying back, so it's a spring idea. Gloves for you, antihistamine nearby, test a patch first.

### Woods at Night
Any of the above outdoors. Lanterns, a fire pit, her led out barefoot in a shift with a rope round her wrists. Location scouting overlap: `Context/Location Scouting.md`.
- Jarvis note: November is cold. Short exposure, warm kit in the car, and a fire pit only on a site where that's actually allowed.
