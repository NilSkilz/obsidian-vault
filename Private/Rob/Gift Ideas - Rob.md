### Black Friday find (27 Nov 2026)
**xTool M1 10W diode (enclosed, with vinyl blade) at GM Crafts: £299, was £699**, "limited qty", free UK delivery. Verified on the GM Crafts Black Friday page 27 Nov. Enclosed, so no DIY enclosure, just duct the exhaust port out (inline fan ~£40). 10W handles 2mm leather. Beats the open-frame plan at the same money. Alternative on the same page: Creality Falcon A1C 10W £296 (was £349). Deal clock: Black Friday sales end ~30 Nov / 1 Dec, so whoever buys it needs to move this weekend.

**Deal check (27 Nov, 11:45):** the "was £699" is a marketing anchor, but £299 is still genuinely cheap. xTool UK's own *refurbished* 10W bundles were listed at £359-399 (and out of stock), and other retailers listed it new for much more. The M1 is the 2022 model, now being cleared out because the M1 Ultra replaced it. Compared with the alternatives: the Creality Falcon A1 10W (newer, enclosed, built-in air assist) is ~£412-429 in the UK; the A1C at £296 is the smaller budget box; and an open-frame Sculpfun plus a DIY enclosure plus glasses ends up costing more. The M1's weak spot is that it has no built-in air assist. That matters for clean leather cuts, so budget about £89 for the xTool air assist add-on later. Verdict given to Rob: buy it.
