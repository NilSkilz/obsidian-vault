# Aimee birthday rope set (gift, 11 Oct 2026)

Teal rope set for Aimee's birthday, dyed and conditioned by Rob. Process reference (shared, no gift mention): `Reference/Rope - conditioning and dyeing.md`. Gift framing moved here 8 Sep 2026 because the Reference file is Aimee-visible.

**Material correction (9 Sep 2026, Rob pulled me up on it):** the reel and every test hank below are **JUTE, not hemp.** Entries dated before this say "hemp" and "hemp's yellow base"; read jute throughout. The chemistry and every number below are unaffected (both are bast/cellulose fibre), but stop calling it hemp, it is the second time it has needed correcting. See [[Rope - conditioning and dyeing]].

- **8 Sep 2026:** the Rope Locker 220m hemp reel arrived. Rob cut 3m test lengths and put them to soak. Test dye baths today: 50/50 and 75/25 turquoise/cerulean, 1L each, **1m of rope (~28-30g) per bath** (corrected from an initial 3m assumption).
- Per-1L bath numbers (corrected for 1m/~28g): total dye ~1g, salt ~28g (2 flat tbsp), soda ash ~5g (1 flat tsp). Salt and soda follow the water volume, dye follows fibre weight. Depth-of-shade target ~3.5% (same as the 1 tsp per 85g recipe), so scale dye linearly with rope weight for the full set.
- **Rob has 0.01g scales, so doses are weighed, not spooned:** 75/25 = 0.75g turquoise + 0.25g cerulean; 50/50 = 0.50g each. Weigh on tared paper (fine staticky powder), paste each dose with warm water before adding to avoid dark speckles.
- Background: earlier jute tests dried green (jute's yellow under turquoise). Fix = add red-shade blue. On-hand blue is Cerulean (Blue MX-G, green-shade, blunter than ideal); computed target was ~70/30 turquoise/cerulean, so the 75/25 bath is the ratio to beat. If both still dry green, buy Mixing Blue MX-R.
- **9 Sep 2026: even 100% cerulean dried too green.** Confirms the diagnosis: Cerulean IS Blue MX-G, a green-shade blue (Paula Burch's pure-MX chart calls it "slightly greenish blue"), so on hemp's yellow base it can never land clean blue. Rob considered "Aquamarine" (Jacquard 069): rejected, it is a mixture absent from the pure-dye list, aqua = turquoise-family, i.e. greener and lighter than cerulean, would make it worse.
- **The buy: Royal Blue (Blue MX-R)**, the red-shade mixing blue whose red lean cancels the fibre's yellow (per the pburch chart). Source check 9 Sep: George Weil came to £17.90 delivered (£10 pot + £7.90 postage) and Amazon has no verifiable MX-R (Jacquard don't publish MX codes), so the plan is **Kemtex "Procion Royal Blue MX-R (1012)"**, 25g £4.80 or 50g £9.00, both in stock, postage on top at checkout (not published, expect a few quid). 25g is plenty: tests use ~1g/bath. Next tests once it arrives: turquoise/royal at 80/20 and 65/35, same 1m/1L/1g-dye protocol, judge dry after a rinse.
- **Full-set scaling (worked out 9 Sep, for when the ratio is confirmed):** 6 x 8m = 48m at ~29g/m = ~1.4kg rope. At the test's ~3.5% depth of shade that is **~48g total dye** (80/20 = 38g turquoise + 10g royal; 65/35 = 31g + 17g). The 25g Royal Blue pot covers either ratio; turquoise needs most of a 50g pot, check stock before ordering from Kemtex (their turquoise is pot 1004). Water scales practically, not literally: **~20L** in a trug/storage box is enough if the rope moves freely, with **salt ~560g** (28g/L) and **soda ash ~100g** (5g/L, dissolved first). Dye all six lengths in ONE bath so the set matches, fan the rope loosely and turn often (clumps = patchy colour). Do NOT run the big bath until a 1m turquoise/royal test has dried clean blue.
- **11 Sep 2026: Rob bought "Royal Navy" dye from Amazon** instead of the Kemtex MX-R plan. MX code unverified (Amazon listings don't publish them) and navy is normally a heavily-loaded mix, so it may not be pure Blue MX-R. Test bath to find out: **pure Royal Navy, 1.00g dye / 1L / 1m rope**, salt 28g up front, soda ash 5g (dissolved) after 15 min, 40°C, hour, keep moving. Verdict is the HUE when rinsed and dry: if the green is gone, the dye works and depth is just more grams; navy loading means 1g may dry mid-blue rather than navy, which is fine for the test. If it still dries green, back to Kemtex 1012.
- **11 Sep 2026 result: the Amazon "Royal Navy" turned out to BE the Royal Blue (MX-R) plan** (listing name vs product confusion, resolved). The 1g pure test **dried clean blue, no hint of yellow/green.** Diagnosis confirmed end to end: red-shade blue cancels hemp's yellow. No Kemtex order needed.
- **Next step: turquoise/royal mix tests, 80/20 and 65/35** (weighed: 0.80g + 0.20g, and 0.65g + 0.35g per 1L/1m bath, same salt/soda/temp protocol). Cerulean is retired from the recipe: it is the green-shade blue that caused the problem, mixing it back in reintroduces green and mutes the shade. Third option to keep in mind: if the pure royal test hank already looks right dry, dye the whole set pure royal and skip mixing entirely (simplest to reproduce at 20L scale). Whichever wins, re-check the full-set gram split above against the chosen ratio.
- **13 Sep 2026: set resized to 4 x 8m** (Rob buying a dye container). 32m at ~29g/m = **~930g rope**. Bath: **15L water** (12L is the workable minimum, 15L gives room to fan the rope out; more water only costs extra salt/soda, it does not improve the colour). Container wants **30L+ capacity**, i.e. a 40L flexi builders' tub or a ~50L storage box; rectangular beats round because the rope can lie in loose coils instead of clumping. Salt **420g** (28g/L, dissolved first), soda ash **75g** (5g/L, dissolved in hot water separately, in after 15 min). Total dye at the test's 3.5% depth of shade = **~33g**: 80/20 = 26g turquoise + 7g royal; 65/35 = 21g + 12g; pure royal = 33g. All four lengths in ONE bath so the set matches. Full bath is ~16kg, so set it up where it will stay (bath tub or floor), and pre-heat with kettle + hot tap since 15L of thin plastic loses heat fast; stand it in a bath of warm water if it drifts under 40.
- **13 Sep 2026, revised again: 4 x 8m plus 4 x 2m** (32m + 8m = **40m, ~1.16kg**). Bath goes to **18L**: salt **500g**, soda ash **90g**, total dye **~41g** (80/20 = 33g turquoise + 8g royal; 65/35 = 27g + 14g; pure royal = 41g). **Turquoise is the supply risk** at 33g, weigh what is left in the pot before committing to the 80/20 ratio; royal is comfortable at either. A 40L flexi tub still works (18L leaves headroom), and the short 2m lengths go in the same bath so they match, threaded loosely, not bundled.
- **16 Sep 2026: dye day, final numbers.** Rob weighed the actual set: **41m = 720g** (~17.6g/m, lighter than the ~29g/m estimate, so dye needs drop). Colour decided: **pure Royal Blue (MX-R), no mixing.** Bath: **18L water, 500g salt (dissolved first), 25g royal blue dye** (3.5% depth of shade on 720g), rope in 15 min with stirring, then **90g soda ash** (dissolved separately in hot water) in, 60 min at ~40°C, stir every few minutes. All lengths in the one bath, fanned loose. Rinse to nearly clear, hot soapy soak, rinse again, crocking test on damp white cloth before it touches skin. Supply check: if the Amazon pot is a standard Jacquard 19g jar it is ~6g short of full depth; 19g still gives a solid mid royal (~2.6% DoS), or top up from Kemtex 1012 (25g £4.80).
- Judge colour DRY, not wet. Hemp dyes darker than jute did.
- Temp lesson from the 8 Sep test bath: target 40°C, but drifting to 50°C isn't a problem, especially pre-soda-ash (dye barely reacts without the fixer). Turquoise actually likes it warmer (some dyers run it 50°C+ on purpose); cerulean is fine too. Post-soda-ash a hot/alkaline bath just spends dye into the water faster, so worst case is slightly paler, not ruined. Soda ash itself is very forgiving, a few times over does no harm; the only real risks are a big excess (paler result) or undissolved granules (dark speckles) so dissolve it first.
- **18 Sep 2026: dye done, drying.** Full set dyed pure Royal Blue on 16 Sep, currently drying under tension on the suspension hoist (which is why the hoist fault fix is paused). Not bone dry yet as of Friday morning. Butane torch arriving today (Amazon) for singeing the fuzz off. Order of operations: fully dry first (jute holds water, 24-48h), then singe (quick passes through the blue cone, keep it moving, wipe down after), THEN oil. Never flame after oiling.
