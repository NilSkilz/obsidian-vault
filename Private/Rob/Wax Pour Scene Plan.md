Scene planned for the Halloween weekend (31 Oct 2026): wax pour on Aimee using a temp-controlled wax jug, ladled on, with a pop-up coffin prop doing double duty as a spill tray.

**Wax/temp decisions:**
- Use soy wax (has paraffin too, but paraffin runs too hot for a ladle pour unless labelled low-melt/body-safe)
- Jug around 50-55°C as a starting point, wrist-test every batch and after any standing time
- Height controls heat on landing: start 30cm+, come lower once calibrated
- Oil skin first (baby oil / almond / jojoba, light sheen) for clean peel-off; oil kills latex, so plan condom/toy use around that
- Candle dye chips/liquid dye for colour; no crayons (raises melting point), no fragrance oil (irritant)

**Theme/design settled on:** bleeding pentagram on her back (black lines, red running off the points) as the lead idea, paired with a "play dead" corpse rule (no moving/sound, blindfold, lily on chest). Narrow spout/small jug for clean pentagram lines rather than the ladle. Outline marked in eyeliner first. UV-reactive dye + blackout + UV torch as a reveal option.

**Coffin prop logistics:** line with a dust sheet (wax/oil will stain the prop's polyester), LED candles only (not real flame) around it, lid stays open so she can sit up/out if anything spikes, confirm in advance she's into being boxed in rather than claustrophobic about it.

**LED candles:** bulk online is the cheap route (eBay 100-packs ~£14, Amazon 24-packs ~£8.50); get warm-white flickering, not cold-white; mix in a couple of pillar candles for a wake look rather than restaurant-table tea lights; red ones to suit the pentagram.
