Planned 20 Nov 2026 for kink club on Sat 21 Nov. Rob's base: Aimee on the lego mat, hands tied to the ceiling, shock collars on her legs. Wanted more wow and a twist on fire play. Built on her love of the witch genre (see [[aimee-scene-ideas]], witch-trial batch). Private: half the value is surprise.

## The frame: a witch trial in three acts
1. **The Accusation (lights low).** Read the charge aloud, short and theatrical. She stands on the lego "for her sins". Hands go up.
2. **The Interrogation (collars).** Each question she won't "confess" to earns a zap. Lifting a foot off the lego earns a zap. Optional: two watchers she's pre-approved hold the remotes as "the jury" (Rob sets the levels, they only press).
3. **The Mark (fire).** Blindfold on. Paint a pentagram/sigil on her back in isopropyl and light it: the devil's mark flashes up across her back in blue-orange and is gone in a second. Then the verdict.

## Wow layers
- **Fire as the only light** for act 3. Ask the DM to kill the spot over your corner.
- **Click roulette:** blindfolded, a Zippo flick could mean flame or zap. She can't tell which is coming.
- **Burn the confession:** she "signs" a confession (or you read one), it goes up in the lidded metal bowl. All flame, none on her.
- **Flash paper/flash cotton** from your hand as the sentence is pronounced (magic-shop stuff, burns instantly, near-zero ash). Never on her skin.
- Optional pre-scene: UV body paint sigils that only show under a UV torch.

## Safety (the non-negotiables)
- **Arms overhead + pain + standing on lego = faint risk.** If she drops she hangs by the wrists (nerve damage). Use padded cuffs to a quick-release snap, not wraps of jute. Cap overhead time ~15-20 min, check hands/colour/pins-and-needles, sit her down between acts if needed. Shears on you.
- **Rope and fire don't meet.** Oiled jute is a wick. Hands-up means rope is above her head and fire stays below the shoulder blades. Hair up, ideally damped.
- **Fuel discipline:** paint thin with a brush, nothing runs. Run-off would reach the collars' contacts and the plastic lego mat (melts/burns). Fuel bowl lidded, well away from her feet.
- **Collars: thighs/calves only**, never neck (below-waist rule). No zaps while fire is on her skin: a flinch while lit is how it spreads.
- **Kit:** 70% isopropyl, damp towels, fire blanket, wand/brush, lidded metal bowl, lighter. Spotter with the blanket who isn't playing. Extinguisher located on arrival.
- **Club:** confirm fire play and electro are allowed at that venue and whether fire needs DM sign-off/a booked slot.
- **Consent:** fire and the jury idea need her yes in advance. The sigil reveal and the theatre can stay a surprise.
- Aftercare: water, sugar, blanket. Check the back for any red patches (burn check) and her hands.
