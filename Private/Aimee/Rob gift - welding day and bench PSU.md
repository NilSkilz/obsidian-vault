# Rob gift shortlist: welding day + bench power supply

**Private to Aimee. Do not write any of this into shared vault files or anywhere Rob reads.**
Researched 13 Sep 2026.

## Why these two

Rob designs steel furniture around bolt-together fittings specifically because he can't weld, so a welding day unlocks something he currently designs around. A bench power supply is a bench tool, not a project part: it protects every board he solders (saline pump ESP32, edge-o-matic sensor build, the AliExpress motor/servo stuff) and gives a live current readout for finding shorts. He already owns a 12V brick, so the PSU is quality-of-life, not a blocker.

## Option A: welding day

**Top pick. The Weld Space, Studio 1 Candlewick Lane, Alma Yard, Plymouth PL4 0FF.** Roughly 1h30 drive from Crackington Haven. Devon Tourism Award winner, courses run through Eventbrite.

Furniture Fabrication Skills is the sharpest match: he goes home with a welded steel stool, which is exactly the thing he keeps designing in bolt-together form.

Gift vouchers: buy any value, recipient redeems against a course on Eventbrite. Good if the date matters more than the course. No stated expiry, worth confirming by email.

**Alternatives considered:**
- Cornwall College (Camborne / St Austell), Welding Skills ARC/MIG evening course, £410 adult tuition. Multi-week evening commitment, an hour-plus each way. Real qualification value, poor gift.
- Red Letter Days full day ARC and MIG class, bookable nationally. Generic, no fabrication outcome.
- Rob Buckley Welding, north Dorset. Well reviewed, too far.
- Cornish Blacksmiths / craftcourses blacksmithing days are closer but they are forge work, not welding. Different skill.

## Option B: bench power supply

What it does: you set a voltage and a current ceiling. If the board tries to draw more than the ceiling, the supply clamps instead of delivering it, so a first power-up either works or just sits at the limit rather than cooking a MOSFET.

| Model | Spec | Approx UK price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tenma 72-10480 | 30V / 3A, single | ~£50 to £75 (CPC Farnell) | Plenty for ESP32 and small 12V pumps. Best value |
| Tenma 72-10495 | 30V / 5A, dual output | ~£100 to £120 (CPC Farnell) | Two independent rails, useful for 5V and 12V at once |
| Korad KA3005P | 30V / 5A, programmable, USB/RS232 | ~£110 to £140 (Amazon UK) | The hobbyist default. PC controllable, 4 memory presets |
| Korad KA3005D | same hardware, no USB | ~£90 to £110 | Save the money if he won't script it |
| Rigol DP711 | 30V / 5A, very low noise | £200+ | Overkill unless he does analogue or RF |

Buying notes: some EU listings ship with a Schuko plug, check for a UK lead. CPC Farnell prices are usually quoted ex VAT, add 20%. I could not pull a live Amazon price, so treat the Korad figures as a range and check on the day.
