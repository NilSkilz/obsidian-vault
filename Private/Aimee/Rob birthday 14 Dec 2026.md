Private to Aimee. Rob never reads this folder.

Shortlist pulled from the shared vault (Projects/, Reference/) on 13 Sep 2026. Everything below is something he has actually written down wanting, or an obvious gap in kit he's using right now.

## The big one: bench power supply, current-limited (~£60-90)

He's mid-way through building the Saline Pump PCB by hand (board arrived, soldering started 10 Sep, bring-up stages 1-7 in progress). Every stage of that involves putting 12V into a board he just soldered and hoping. A bench PSU with an adjustable current limit is the single tool that turns "I let the smoke out" into "it clamped at 200mA and nothing died".

Look for: Korad KA3005P, RD6006, or a Tenma/RS equivalent. 30V/5A is plenty. Anything with a current-limit knob and a display.

Why it's a good gift: he'll use it on every project he has going (saline pump, the fucking machine motor, the ESP32 builds), and it's the sort of thing people don't buy themselves because it isn't blocking anything today.

## Soldering kit around the new iron (~£40-90, bundles nicely)

He bought a **Pinecil V2** on 10 Sep and started using it immediately. It runs off USB-C PD, which opens up some genuinely good accessories:

- **100W USB-C PD power bank** (~£50-70). Turns the Pinecil cordless. He can solder in the garage, in the van, on the floor next to the rig. This is the one that would actually delight.
- **Extra cartridge tips**, especially a 2mm chisel (his notes say chisel, not needle, for this board's through-hole work) plus a couple of finer ones for later.
- **Silicone soldering mat**, **PCB vice or helping hands**, **fume extractor fan**. Cheap, all missing from his notes, all annoying to not have.

## Pipe cutter / metalwork for the Scaffold Rig (£20-35)

He researched and picked, but I can't tell if he's bought:
- **Faithfull 6-42mm manual steel pipe cutter, Screwfix £20.99, code 161YL**. His chosen tool for cutting 33.7mm mild steel tube.
- Fallback he costed: **Erbauer 750W 115mm angle grinder, £32.99** plus 1mm cutting discs and a flap disc.
- Worth checking his workshop before buying. Note he specifically ruled out the Rothenberger £109.99 one (thin-wall only) and said Toolstation is a dead end.

Also in that world: a **heat gun** (~£25-40) if he doesn't have one. He settled on heat-curing boiled linseed oil on the bare steel, which turns a three-day job into an afternoon.
