# Tuesday 8 September 2026: No-contact day, sorting the feelings, shared log request

**His morning message:** The standard "morning, love you", which felt ridiculous after a fight and while not talking.

**The feelings, untangled:** Aimee spent the day feeling like crying without quite knowing why, then separated it into three strands:
1. Drop after a great weekend.
2. Jealousy/insecurity about Mary.
3. Hurt about the broken boundaries, the lack of regard for them, and Rob's inability to acknowledge that he does this.

**What she needs:** Stability. She can put up with Rob being entirely focused on his latest project (whether that's an AI or a new girlfriend), but she doesn't feel she's getting anything out of the relationship. Security and honesty don't feel like a lot to ask.

- "No reassurance other than explaining how I get things wrong": she arrived with a sorted, non-accusatory account of her feelings and got a correction instead of comfort. Pattern-adjacent to the sulk on the 6th, her vulnerability meeting deflection.
- The shared boundaries log: a concrete, reasonable ask, and directly aimed at the recurring problem (his account vs her memory, e.g. the Sunday "no" about seeing Mary). His counter (a private log only he can see) doesn't solve the problem the log exists to solve: shared facts.
- The ask itself is worth holding onto as her clearest statement yet of the minimum: security and honesty.
