# ENM Journal

Aimee's private log of the ENM journey. Lives in Private/Aimee, which Rob never reads. Nothing goes in here unless Aimee tells Jarvis to log it, and nothing in here is ever repeated to Rob or written anywhere he reads.

Alongside the dated entries, `Play dates log.md` is a one-line-per-event ledger of play dates and time away, for quick pull-up without rereading whole entries.

## How it works

- One file per entry, named by date: `2026-09-09.md`. Multiple entries in a day get a suffix (`2026-09-09 b.md`).
- Aimee messages Jarvis whatever she wants logged, in whatever state (bullet points, voice-note ramble, half a thought). Jarvis writes it up as an entry and confirms.
- Entries stay in Aimee's own words as much as possible. Jarvis tidies, never editorialises.

## Entry template

```markdown
# <date> — <one-line summary>

**Who/what/where:**

**What happened:**

**Conversations:** (what was said, what was agreed, anything to revisit)

**How I'm feeling:**

**To talk about / follow up:**
```

Sections are optional, use what fits. Skip anything that doesn't apply.
