**Description:**

Proper rope takes time. Every length here starts as raw untreated jute and goes through the full traditional process by hand: hot-washed to strip the mill oils, dried under tension so it hangs dead straight, flame-singed to burn off the fuzz, conditioned with camellia oil, then broken in by hand. It arrives soft and ready to tie, not stiff as string.

Dyed ropes use fibre-reactive Procion dye, the only type that chemically bonds to plant fibre. Cheap dyed rope uses all-purpose dye that sits on the surface and rubs off on skin and sheets. Every dyed rope here is crock-tested (rubbed hard on white cloth) before it ships.

SPECS
- 100% natural jute, approx 8mm diameter (a satisfying, grippy tie, kinder on skin than thin rope)
- True 8m (26ft) finished length. Jute shrinks when conditioned, so each rope is cut well oversize raw; you get the full 8m, measured after treatment
- Approx 150g per 8m rope
- Ends whipped so they can never unravel
- Colours: Natural (golden tan), Teal, Royal Blue

CARE
Keep it dry, store loose or hanked, re-oil lightly with camellia or jojoba every few months. Treated jute gets better with use.

IMPORTANT
Natural-fibre rope, sold for floor work and decorative/rope-art use. Not load-rated: never use for suspension or supporting body weight overhead. By purchasing you confirm you are 18+ and accept responsibility for safe use.

Made to order in the UK. Dispatched in 3-5 working days (the drying stage alone takes two days, and it's worth it).

4-rope sets with a cotton storage bag and tested safety shears also available; message me or see my other listings.
