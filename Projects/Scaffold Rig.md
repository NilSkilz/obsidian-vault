# Scaffold Rig

Modular key-clamp scaffold play furniture. One parts box strips down to a shared base, then rebuilds as either a **whipping post** or a **spanking / whipping bench** with an allen key. Blueprint-style orthographic design plus a photoreal render both exist on disk (see Assets).

Built from **48.3mm O/D galvanised tube** with **cast key-clamp fittings** (grub-screw / allen-key clamps, not threaded pipe). Scaffold key-clamp is the right call here: 48mm tube barely notices lateral pull, which is exactly what a whipping post takes.

## A · Whipping post
Single galv upright, raked scaffold brace triangulated with a swivel clamp, top spreader/anchor bar with a D-ring, flanged base.

- Overall height ~**2300mm**; wrist/anchor point ~**2100mm**
- Base depth ~**1020mm**
- Fittings: **T35** side-outlet tee at the apex (carries the anchor bar), **T25** side-outlet elbow, **T15** 90° elbow, **T55** single swivel for the raked tie, **C50** railing flange to bolt the post to the floor.

## B · Spanking / whipping bench
Sloped top: low knee pad at the front, taller chest pad at the rear (kneel front, torso over the rear). Black leather / wipe-clean PU with contrast stitching, D-rings at the corners, diagonal swivel brace.

- Front (knee) height ~**400mm**; rear (chest) height ~**760mm**
- Length ~**900mm**
- Fittings: **T20** tees where legs meet the platform rail, **T25** elbows, **T55** swivel brace.

## C · Shared base
Rectangular tube footprint both configs plug into. Strip to this, rebuild either rig.

- **600mm** wide × **900mm** deep
- Post socket via **T26** three-socket tee; bench legs via **T25 / T26**.

## Fittings legend
- **T15** — 90° elbow: plain corner
- **T20** — tee: leg meets platform rail
- **T25** — side-outlet elbow: 3-way corner, upright rises
- **T26** — three-socket tee: through rail + vertical socket
- **T35** — side-outlet tee: apex, carries anchor bar
- **T55** — single swivel: any angle (raked braces)
- **C50** — railing flange: bolt post to floor (max lateral resistance)

## Build notes
- One parts box, allen key rebuild between the two configs.
- Swivel fittings (T55) do the triangulation — this is where scaffold beats threaded pipe.
- Whipping post is demountable as drawn. **For suspension-adjacent loads**, bolt the base with C50 flanges into joists/masonry and **derate to the fittings, not the tube.**
- Dimensions sized for a ~5'8"–6'0" body; tweak leg lengths to fit the user.
- **All dims indicative — verify before build.**
