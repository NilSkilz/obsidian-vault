## Build status (BUILT + LIVE 24 Sep 2026, ~22:35)

The whole pipeline shipped in one evening. Live at **https://cracky.co.uk/finances** (parents-only, same wall as health/journal).

1. **Schema** in the family SQLite (mission-control `server/lib/financeDb.js`): financeAccounts, financeTransactions (signed pence, upsert on account+providerTxnId), financeBalances (daily snapshots), financeAssets + financeAssetValues (dated series), financeMortgages. Seeds for all of Rob's 24 Sep numbers are idempotent in the module.
2. **API** `server/routes/finance.js` (`/api/finance`): `overview` (net worth, amortised mortgage positions incl. months-to-rate-cliff + payoff date + equity, 12mo cash flow excluding category='internal', recent), `transactions` (paged/searchable), `ingest` (Jarvis key only), `asset-value` (true-ups; parents or Jarvis).
3. **Page** `src/tide/TideFinances.jsx`: stat tiles (net worth £85,083 at launch), account cards + spaces, in/out bar chart (CVD-validated blue #5a8fd6 / coral #d96d8f), mortgage cards with rate-cliff pill, tap-to-true-up assets (pensions show "add"), recent transactions.

Starling last 8 days + balances + spaces; Monzo with refresh-token rotation (rewrites monzo.env), rolling 90 days, declines skipped, pot transfers marked internal. `--backfill` mode loaded the full dumps: **22,920 transactions ingested** (Starling personal 1,256 + joint 7,823 dumped to `~/finance/starling/`, Monzo 14,335 minus declines).

### Round 3, 24 Sep ~23:00 (income undercount fixed + P&L)

- **Rob spotted the cash-flow chart showing a permanent heavy deficit (~£2k/mo, £23k/yr).** Root cause was NOT double-counted spend: it was uncounted income. The round-2 own-names rule excluded ANY transfer with a Stokes counterparty, and **Aimee's wages arrive into the joint as counterparty "Amy Stokes" (desc "Aimee wages", £1,350-1,450/mo, £16,887.50 over 12 months)** because her wage account isn't one of our feeds. Same for the odd inbound from Rob's unfeeded accounts.
- **Fix: transfer-leg pairing.** An own-name transaction is `internal` only when the opposite leg is visible in the batch (same amount, opposite sign, different account, within 4 days, greedy nearest-time match in `finance-sync.py pair_own_transfers`). Unpaired own-name credits become `income`; unpaired debits become `transfer` (money leaving the tracked system, counts as out). Boundary accounting: cash flow = flows across the edge of the accounts we can see. Consequence for the sync: the Starling nightly window is now 89 days to match Monzo, so a re-ingested transaction always has its twin in the batch (otherwise its category would flip on re-sync).
- **Transactions now carry `source`** (Starling source / Monzo scheme: DIRECT_DEBIT, SUBSCRIPTION_CHARGE, bacs...), column added via guarded migration; full history re-pushed (22,837 rows all sourced).
- **New `/api/finance/pnl`**: monthly P&L, income grouped Rob (Supergroup/Aperture/own-name credits) / Aimee / Other, spending grouped mortgages, bills & subscriptions (DD + subscription source or bills categories + Parentpay), savings & investments (NS&I/Wealthify), groceries, eating out, transport & fuel, shopping, entertainment, holidays, everything else; per-payee rows inside each group; summary = 6-full-month averages. (Gotcha fixed same night: income and spend group keys share one aggregation map, so income keys are namespaced `in_*`.)
- **/finances page: collapsible P&L section** (month picker, columns = selected month + 6-mo avg, tap a group for payee rows, net saved/overspend line) + "min monthly bills" stat tile. **Demoted 24 Sep round 4** behind a collapsed "monthly detail" toggle; the headline view is now:

- **Morrisons fuel was hiding in groceries (Rob asked how to differentiate).** The Bude petrol-station kiosk terminal is ref **'MORR BUDE'** under the plain 'Morrisons' counterparty, and Starling tags it GROCERIES (the store is 'MORRISONS BUDE - 325' / 'WM MORRISONS STORE'; the pay-at-pump route comes through as counterparty 'Morrisons Petrol'). Same normalisation added to `finance-sync.py starling_txn` so nightly re-ingests don't flip it back (ingest upserts overwrite category), and `pnlGroup` routes any 'petrol' counterparty to Transport as a belt-and-braces.
