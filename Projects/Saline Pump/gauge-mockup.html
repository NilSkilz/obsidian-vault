TFT reality check. Everything here maps to what a GC9A01 can draw with TFT_eSPI: the liquid is a full-screen 240×240 sprite, filled column-by-column (blue below the waterline, dark slate above), the waterline is two summed sine terms for the overlapping-wave look, phase advanced each frame. Dose ring = fillArc. Text = large GFX font pushed onto the same sprite, then one pushSprite per frame (no flicker). Colours drop to RGB565. The round glass clips the corners for free.
