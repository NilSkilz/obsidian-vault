# Jarvis Productisation (idea)

**Status:** idea only, raised 14 Sep 2026. Not a commitment, not a third project.

## The idea

Rob floated selling what we've built together (a personal AI assistant that runs projects like [[Tide]], calendar integration, nudges, memory) as a real product or service. Unsure how to sell or monetise it.

## Jarvis's take (given 14 Sep 2026)

- The software isn't the product (mostly Claude Code + glue scripts, and Anthropic keeps eating the generic-assistant space). What's sellable is the expertise: setup, taste, a year of learned lessons about what to automate and what to leave alone.
- Sell as a productised service, not a licence:
  - Fixed-price install: private AI chief-of-staff on the buyer's own hardware, data never leaves the house. £1.5-3k, ~a week of evenings, repeatable playbook.
  - Monthly retainer for care and feeding (recurring revenue, where the margin lives).
  - Or aim at small businesses instead of households ("ops assistant for your 5-person company"), £500/month is a rounding error for them.
- Cheapest test: one evening building a landing page + sanitised write-up, see if anyone bites, before building anything real.

## Cautions

- Can't be project number three alongside [[Tethered]] (mid-pivot) and the contract hunt. Finite evenings.
- Best near-term use is as **positioning for the contract hunt**: "I build agentic AI systems in production" is a strong, differentiated line in the 2026 contract market, and it's backed by a working demo (this vault/setup). Free money, not new work.
- Don't undersell as "a consultant who knows AI" — lead with "ran one in production for a year."
