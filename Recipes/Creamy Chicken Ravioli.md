# Creamy Chicken Ravioli

One pan plus a pot, about 25 minutes, serves 4. No scales needed.

## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
- 2 x 250g packs fresh ravioli (cheese, spinach & ricotta, or mushroom)
- 1 tbsp oil and a knob of butter
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 small onion or 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 300ml double cream (one small pot)
- 100ml chicken stock (half a stock cube in boiling water)
- Big handful of grated parmesan (about 4 heaped tbsp)
- 2 big handfuls of spinach, or a handful of halved cherry tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs or oregano, salt, pepper

## Method
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and the herbs. Fry in the oil over a medium-high heat for 6 to 8 mins until golden and cooked through. Lift out onto a plate.
2. Same pan, lower the heat, add the butter and onion. Soften for 3 mins, then the garlic for 1 min.
3. Pour in the stock and scrape up the brown bits. Add the cream, simmer 3 to 4 mins until it coats a spoon. Stir in the parmesan.
4. Meanwhile, cook the ravioli in salted boiling water (fresh takes 2 to 4 mins). Drain gently, keep a mug of the pasta water.
5. Chicken back into the sauce, add the spinach (or tomatoes) and let it wilt. Fold in the ravioli. Loosen with a splash of pasta water if it's thick.
6. Pepper and extra parmesan on top.

## Notes
- Lighter swap: replace the cream with a 400g tin of chopped tomatoes plus 2 tbsp cream cheese.
- Leftover roast chicken works: shred it and add at step 5.
- Kid-friendly as is; add chilli flakes to adult portions.
