# Cocoa Brownies

20cm square tin, lined. 160°C fan.

## Ingredients

- 140g butter, melted
- 250g caster sugar
- 75g cocoa
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 65g plain flour
- Pinch of salt
- Optional: a spoonful of demerara on top for a crackly lid

## Method

1. Stir the cocoa and sugar into the warm melted butter.
2. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
3. Fold in the flour and salt.
4. Scatter demerara over the top if using.
5. Bake 20 to 25 mins. Pull them out while the middle still has a slight wobble.
