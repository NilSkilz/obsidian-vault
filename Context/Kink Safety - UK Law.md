# Kink Safety - UK Law

Legal reference on where private kink practice and images cross into criminal territory. Not legal advice, just the practical lines, checked 3 Nov 2026.

## Images

- Crime and Policing Act 2026: from **Friday 6 Nov 2026**, possessing or publishing porn that realistically shows strangulation or suffocation is an offence (England and Wales). Pre-existing saved breath-play images are not exempt, clear them out before that date.
- Extreme porn law (2008) only catches realistic images of: an act threatening life, serious injury to anus/breasts/genitals, bestiality, necrophilia, or rape. Ordinary spanking/caning/flogging images are legal to own; heavy CBT or breast torture imagery is where it starts getting close.

## Doing it (the part that actually matters)

- **R v Brown (1993):** consent is no defence to actual bodily harm or worse. ABH = injury more than "transient or trifling".
- **Domestic Abuse Act 2021, s71:** consenting "for sexual gratification" is no defence to ABH+ between partners.
- Practical line: a red mark that fades by the next day is fine. Welts, broken skin, or bruising lasting days is technically ABH regardless of consent. Prosecutions are rare absent a complaint, but risk spikes exactly when a relationship or scene goes bad.
- Filming privately is fine; selling or publishing brings in Ofcom/Online Safety Act rules separately.

## Permanent marking (branding / scarification, 7 Nov 2026)

- **Amateur branding is a no.** A **heat gun** can't do it (blown air tops out ~600C, a solid iron soaks it unevenly, so you hold it on longer and cook deeper tissue: a bigger, uglier, more infection-prone wound, not a crisper mark). A **propane torch** fixes the temperature but nothing else.
- **The Amazon "custom branding iron" is the wrong tool** whatever heats it: solid brass, made for wood/leather/steak, with fine detail. A brand scar spreads to 2-3x the line width as it heals, so close-set lines blob, and keloiding is a real risk. Pros use **strike branding** (thin steel strips, built up one quick touch at a time) or electrocautery.
- **It's a body-mod procedure, not a scene prop:** a third-degree burn with weeks of wound aftercare. If a permanent mark is genuinely wanted, a scarification artist does it.
- **Legally** it's R v Brown territory above: consent doesn't reliably cover permanent bodily harm done by an amateur.
- **Consent standard:** permanent means a sober, considered yes given well before and outside any scene. A yes in headspace does not count for this.
- **The safe substitute** (used in the hot-iron scene idea): torch an iron where she can see and hear it, blindfold, then press an identical **freezer-cold** iron. It reads as a burn for a split second. Freezer cold only, never dry ice or colder, or you've given a real cold burn. Hot iron into a sand bucket before the cold one appears.
