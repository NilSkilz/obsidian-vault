# Kink Safety - UK Law

Legal reference on where private kink practice and images cross into criminal territory. Not legal advice, just the practical lines, checked 3 Nov 2026.

## Images

- Crime and Policing Act 2026: from **Friday 6 Nov 2026**, possessing or publishing porn that realistically shows strangulation or suffocation is an offence (England and Wales). Pre-existing saved breath-play images are not exempt, clear them out before that date.
- Extreme porn law (2008) only catches realistic images of: an act threatening life, serious injury to anus/breasts/genitals, bestiality, necrophilia, or rape. Ordinary spanking/caning/flogging images are legal to own; heavy CBT or breast torture imagery is where it starts getting close.

## Doing it (the part that actually matters)

- **R v Brown (1993):** consent is no defence to actual bodily harm or worse. ABH = injury more than "transient or trifling".
- **Domestic Abuse Act 2021, s71:** consenting "for sexual gratification" is no defence to ABH+ between partners.
- Practical line: a red mark that fades by the next day is fine. Welts, broken skin, or bruising lasting days is technically ABH regardless of consent. Prosecutions are rare absent a complaint, but risk spikes exactly when a relationship or scene goes bad.
- Filming privately is fine; selling or publishing brings in Ofcom/Online Safety Act rules separately.
