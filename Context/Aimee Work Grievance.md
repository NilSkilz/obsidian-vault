## Why formal, and why now

The informal route is exhausted, and that is now an asset. A formal written grievance triggers the ACAS Code: the employer must investigate, hold a meeting, give a written outcome, and offer an appeal. Failure to follow it can uplift any later tribunal award by up to 25%. The sentence "I raised this informally on [dates] with [names] and the behaviour continued" turns their inaction into the employer's problem.

VetPartners will have a proper group grievance procedure. Get a copy of it (staff handbook / intranet / ask HR for it in writing) and follow it to the letter. If the local directors are too close to the manager to act, the grievance can be escalated to the VetPartners group People team.

## Practical notes

- Send it in writing (email is fine) and keep a copy outside work systems.
- Take a companion to the meeting: statutory right (colleague or union rep).
- If her health is suffering, see the GP and say why; that creates a medical record.
- ACAS helpline for free advice: 0300 123 1100.
- Check home insurance for legal expenses cover (often includes employment disputes).
- Constructive dismissal stays in the back pocket: it means resigning first, needs 2 years' service until Jan 2027 (then 6 months), and threatening it while staying weakens the claim.
