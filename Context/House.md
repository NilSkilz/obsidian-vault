### Battery sizing & payback (worked 17 Sep 2026)
- Derived annual figures (assuming ~2022 install, ~4.25 yrs of data): generation ~3,600 kWh/yr, export ~1,900 kWh/yr (53%), self-used solar ~1,660 kWh/yr, grid import ~4,300 kWh/yr, total consumption ~6,000 kWh/yr (~16.5 kWh/day).
- ~~Step zero, free money: register Octopus Outgoing~~ **VOID (22 Sep 2026): already registered and being paid.** Outgoing Octopus at 12p/kWh (was 15p until 1 Mar 2026), credits on every statement. Nothing was given away and there is no form to fill.
- **Battery economics on today's setup (with SEG at 12p):** solar-shifting value = 26p − 12p = 14p/kWh × ~1,500 kWh captured ≈ £210/yr. On Flexible there's no arbitrage on top. A ~10 kWh AC-coupled system runs **£5,000-7,000 installed** (0% VAT, G99 adds lead time) → payback 12+ years on solar-shifting alone. **Marginal today.**
- **What changes the maths:** a TOU tariff. EV lands (car hunt live) → Intelligent Octopus Go ~7p overnight, charge battery cheap + run the house on it: adds ~£300-450/yr. Heat pump lands → Octopus Cosy does similar. Either takes total benefit to **£500-650/yr, payback ~7-9 yrs** vs 10-12 yr warranty. Octopus Flux is the no-EV/no-HP middle option.
- **Sizing:** ~10 kWh usable is the sweet spot (evening+overnight load, summer export peaks ~10-12 kWh/day); 13.5 kWh class (Powerwall 3, GivEnergy All in One) if future-proofing for heat pump + EV.
- **Recommendation given to Rob 17 Sep (amended 22 Sep, Outgoing already done):** get 2-3 battery quotes now (G99 wait means starting early costs nothing) but time the actual install decision alongside the EV/heat-pump calls, which is what makes the battery pay.

- Solar match: mismatch is seasonal (panels weakest exactly when heating demand peaks, Dec-Feb), but strong for hot water — heating water via the heat pump on solar surplus gets ~3x the yield of an immersion diverter (COP effect), covering most of the household's hot water roughly March-October.
