# Location Scouting

Abandoned and secluded spots good for rope/suspension shoots and atmospheric pics. Standing request from Rob (23 Aug 2026): keep eyes open for places like this and add them here as I find them.

## What makes a spot good (Rob's criteria, inferred from Wenford)

- Large abandoned structures, industrial texture, good light for photos
- Solid overhead anchor options (beams, girders, lintels) if suspension is on the cards
- Secluded enough to rig and shoot undisturbed
- Reachable without a hike-in carrying a full rope kit
- Always Rob's call on anchor safety in person; a listed ruin can look solid and be rotten. Nothing here is a structural sign-off.

## Confirmed good (Rob has used)

### Wenford Dries, Camel Trail (used 23 Aug 2026, suspension + pics)
- Former china clay drying works at Poley's Bridge, near Wenfordbridge, north end of the Camel Trail (Bodmin side). Operated until 2002, now a long run of conjoined granite/brick/concrete drying halls with chimneys, right alongside the trail.
- Rob's verdict: large, abandoned, great for pics. Did a suspension there.
- Note: it sits beside a popular cycle trail, so passing traffic exists; clearly quiet enough in practice.

## Candidates (unverified, from research 23 Aug 2026)

Same clay-country / North Cornwall belt, ordered roughly by how similar they feel to Wenford:

- **Luxulyan Valley (Trevanny clay dry, Ponts Mill, Carmears wheelpit, Treffry Viaduct)**, near St Blazey. Wooded valley full of abandoned clay and mining works, big stone structures, quieter than the coast path. Probably the closest match to Wenford's vibe.
- **Prince of Wales Quarry engine house**, near Tintagel/Trebarwith. Restored-ish engine house in a slate quarry setting, secluded valley.
- **Roche Rock chapel ruin**, Roche. 15th-century chapel built into a granite outcrop, very atmospheric, but exposed and well known, better for pics than play.
- **Kit Hill / Kelly Bray mine stacks**, near Callington. Engine houses and stacks, open access land.
- **Camborne-Pool-Redruth mining corridor**. Dozens of engine houses and ruins; volume play, but further away and some are consolidated/fenced.
- **Bodmin Jail older sections**, Bodmin. Partly restored as an attraction, decayed wings exist, but it's a paid/staffed site, pics only.

To verify before recommending any of these for a session: access rights, how overlooked they are, and whether there's anything rated to hang from.
