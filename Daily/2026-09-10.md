Sent the plan: unplug the pump (winding masks diode readings), autopsy the new Q1 (gate→GND ohms low again = same death; also D-S both ways vs Q2 to rule out an overcurrent death instead, which would point at snubber C2 or wiring), then diode-test U1 vs U2 and reflow U1's legs regardless.
