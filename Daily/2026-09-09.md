- 23:50 Solar + heat pump match (house has PV): genuinely complementary but seasonally lopsided. Winter space heating (the big load) lands when PV output is ~10-15% of summer, so solar barely touches Dec-Feb heating. The year-round win is hot water: the new cylinder is a thermal battery, heat it midday on solar surplus and each solar kWh becomes ~3 kWh of heat via the pump's COP (vs 1:1 with an immersion diverter). Midday running also = warmest air = best COP, and self-using a kWh in the pump beats exporting it at SEG rates. Shoulder seasons PV covers a real slice of heating; "heat the house in the day, coast at night" works partly via thermal mass and is how heat pumps like to run anyway (steady + low). Battery would sharpen it further but isn't needed to make the case.
