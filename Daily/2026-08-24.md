- Rob floated push-button QD sling swivels instead of the eye nuts. Advised against for this build: strength is fine on branded units (Magpul/DD/BFG, 264-450 lb pull-tested; eBay pot-metal pairs are not), but QD sockets only exist as rifle-stock flush cups (1/2"-32 thread or epoxy press-fit), nothing mates with M10 without machining a custom end plug. Deeper issue: push-button QDs bind under tension, so the release is stiffest exactly when someone is panicking and pulling. Recommendation: keep DIN 582 eye nuts, add panic snaps in the chain (designed to release under full load, one-handed, clip straight to the eye nut).
