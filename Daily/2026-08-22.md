### Telegram bridge
- 11:03 Rob reported the bridge "hanging", then "really slow". Investigated properly rather than taking the impression at face value.
  - **Not the bridge, not the box.** Service up since 10:51, container idle (load 0.11, 5.2GB free), usage 17% weekly, no rate-limit throttle. A trivial round-trip through the same binary/model is a consistent 8-9s, so the API isn't degraded either.
  - **It was real, though.** Parsed reply latencies out of `bridge.log` (time from each `<<` to its next `>>`): median 141s today and 121s yesterday, against a 40-70s norm; p90 404s, worst 1054s. The two elevated days are exactly the two days of the Snapcast/Music Assistant dig, which was 30-odd tool calls per message. So: work volume, not latency.
  - **The actual defect was legibility, not speed.** The ack says a run started, then nothing until the summary, so a 10-min investigation and a hung container are indistinguishable from the phone. Since the container really did hang at 10:51, every long pause now reads as death.
  - Fix (commit `b5bb44f`): progress heartbeats at 3 and 10 min that fire *on top of* the ack. The existing `LONG_JOB_NOTICE` couldn't cover this, it only fires when nothing has been said at all, which is never now that streamed acks are the default.
  - Also cut `CLAUDE_TIMEOUT` 25 min -> 15. The measured p99 is under 10 min even on the heaviest days, so the extra 10 was pure dead air on a genuinely wedged run.
  - Restarted 11:24, deliberately after Rob's in-flight Spotify thread closed at 11:22 so it didn't eat his reply.
