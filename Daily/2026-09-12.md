My bug, found and fixed inside the hour. `setup()` armed CHANGE interrupts on all four encoder lines regardless of whether an encoder was plugged in, and Enc R's CLK/DT are GPIO34/35: input-only, no internal pull-up. KY-040s aren't wired yet (they mount to the enclosure still on the printer), so both pins floated, chattered, and the interrupt storm starved `loop()`: no LED heartbeat, no web server, a board that looked bricked. Also moved `QTAB` to DRAM (an IRAM ISR reading a flash table crashes if it fires mid-NVS-write or mid-OTA) and added a three-blink LED burst at the top of `setup()`, so "did it boot at all" no longer needs a serial cable. Compiles clean (81% flash, 16% RAM), page re-verified in headless Chrome.
