16:05 **Stage 9: the flow line gets a second point, and the cells start stopping pumps.** Rob's first real wet calibration caught **140 ml in 60s at 100%**. Then a 200 ml dose at 60% ran for **2:23** and delivered ~140 ml. 2:23 is exactly what 84 ml/min predicts, so the firmware was right and the model was 43% optimistic: **a PWM'd DC peristaltic head is not proportional to duty**, it has to beat its own friction before the tube moves, so flow vs duty is a line crossing zero well above zero duty. Fixed two ways.
- **Two-point flow calibration**, 140 @ 100% and 58.7 @ 60% (~2.03 ml/min per point of duty), both in NVS, four buttons on the phone page (L/R at 100%, L/R at 60%). Rob's 58.7 is derived from that run and ships as the default so the rig is honest on boot, but wants re-measuring with a jug. The page's local tween runs the identical function, so phone and board can't disagree.
- **Doses are now weighed, not timed.** With a trusted base, delivered ml is what has actually left the bag and that is what stops the pump. Four guards make it safer than open loop, not scarier: weighing only arms if cell noise is under 3 g *and* under 5% of the target; a knock can't finish a dose (delivered capped at 1.6x the clock + 15 ml); if the clock passes 2x target while the cell disagrees it stops on a new **CHECK** state rather than a misleading DONE; and a cell going quiet mid-run falls back to the clock with no jump. The gauge says "weighed" or "timed" so it's never a guess.
