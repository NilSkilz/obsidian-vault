- **Evening pivot:** the KiCad/custom-PCB route didn't land for Rob ("didn't rly work, nvm"). He's leaning to an **18650 battery shield** (5V + 12V, rechargeable, ESP over USB) instead of the mains brick + buck. My read: good idea, arguably safer (no mains near a wet scene), and current is trivial (pumps 250–300mA @12V). Caveats: single cell ≈ one run (go 2–3 cells for headroom), and the v2 heater would blow the battery budget. - **Netlist review (Rob drew his own board in Cirkit Designer, sent the `.enet`).** Cross-checked all 20 parts/nets vs the locked v1 pinout. **Signal/logic wiring is spot on** (displays CS L=5/R=4 + shared SPI, encoders 32/33/27 & 14/13/22, HX711 DT 34/35 + shared SCK 21, all 3V3-powered, flybacks oriented right, strapping pins clear). **Two power-section errors flagged before fab:** (1) **critical** — Q1 (pump L) source is tied to Q2's drain (net $1N19, pump R's low side) instead of GND, so the two pump MOSFETs are stacked in series not independent low-side switches; pump L can't run unless pump R's FET is also on. Fix: Q1 source → GND. (2) the 5V buck's 12V input taps the *switched* rail (after the e-stop), so the e-stop kills the ESP32 too, defeating the "stay alive to show STOPPED" design. Fix: buck taps raw 12V *before* the e-stop. Minor: gate 10k pulldowns sit on the MCU side of the 150Ω series R (works, canonical is gate-to-source); ESTOP_SENSE GPIO16 left unwired (optional, fine).
- **Re-review of the corrected `.enet` (Rob sent v2). All three fixes confirmed in the netlist, clear to fab:** (1) Q1.S and Q2.S both now on GND, drains separate ($1N10 pump L, $1N19 pump R) = two independent low-side switches. (2) Buck U2.IN+ now taps raw 12V ($1N37, the input jack U3) *before* the e-stop (U10, in series $1N37→$1N41 pump rail), so e-stop kills the pumps but leaves the ESP32 powered. (3) Both 10k pulldowns (R4/R1) moved to gate-to-source; 150Ω series R (R3/R2) sits ESP→gate. Good board.
