# Scene Idea: The Exorcism of Aimee

*Captured 2026-08-22 from Rob. Status: idea stage, not yet negotiated. Cast would be Rob + Aimee plus friends Luke and Raven (a couple who potentially want to scene with them, first time playing together). Venue: privately hired dungeon.*

## The arc (Rob's outline)

Three acts: medical horror, then exorcism ritual, then release. Aimee is escorted in blindfolded and hooded, restrained throughout.

**Act 1, Medical.** Heart rate monitor (pads + fake beeping audio), ventilator-style mask over mouth. Talk of symptoms, running a fever, ice to cool her. She struggles, gets a fake tranquiliser injection (needle in arm, nothing attached), then is talked about as if unconscious.

**Act 2, Exorcism.** Luke as priest, chanting audio throughout. Wax poured on her back in a star. Raven uses a strapon on her, "fucking the demons out", while Rob and Luke watch and chant. Impact on Raven while she's doing it.

**Act 3, Release.** The demon leaves. Finish with a cum lube injection.

**Hard lines already set:** nothing sexual between Rob and Raven, nothing sexual between Luke and Aimee.

## More medical ideas (Act 1)

She's blindfolded, so sound, smell, and temperature do all the work:

- **Alcohol wipe swab** before the injection. That cold sting and clinical smell is instant hospital.
- **Cold stethoscope** dragged slowly across her chest and back, with worried muttering about what's heard.
- **BP cuff**, inflated properly tight. Genuinely medical, genuinely restrictive, and it ratchets tension.
- **Pen torch pupil checks**: peel the blindfold edge, light in the eye, "non-responsive" verdict she can hear.
- **Pinwheel "reflex tests"** up the soles and inner thighs, noting "abnormal responses" out loud.
- **Rob controls the heartbeat audio.** Speed it up when she's touched, let it stutter at bad news. The monitor becomes a character.
- **Third-person clinical talk.** She is "the patient". Clipboard, observations dictated aloud. Depersonalisation does heavy lifting here.
- **Ice as treatment**: cube traced down the spine, wrapped packs at neck and wrists, "core temperature still climbing" panic.
- **TENS pads as "muscle response testing"** on thighs or calves (the EM43; below-waist placement rule as always).
- **Escalating restraint**: straps added one at a time as she "deteriorates", each one narrated.
- **The bridge to Act 2:** Luke arrives partway through as a consulted specialist. He examines her, goes quiet, then declares this is not medical. That pivot gives Act 2 a reason to exist and gets Luke on stage early instead of waiting in the wings.

## Keeping all four involved

- **Everyone has a job in every act.** Rob: doctor, handler, impact top. Luke: specialist-then-priest, owns audio, chants, wax. Raven: nurse in Act 1 (handing instruments, holding limbs, taking "readings"), the vessel-cleanser in Act 2.
- **The impact on Raven needs a story beat**, or it reads as random. Frame: the demon lashes out at whoever touches the vessel. Raven takes the demon's strikes and keeps going. That makes the impact part of the ritual and keeps her centred rather than sidelined.
- **Write the touch map down beforehand.** Who touches whom, where, with what, per act. The no-Rob/Raven and no-Luke/Aimee lines are set, but wax on Aimee's back from Luke sits near that line, so define it explicitly rather than discovering the edge mid-scene.
- **Negotiate Raven's impact separately.** Rob hasn't played with her before. Start light, calibrate, agree her tolerances and no-go zones in advance.
- **Pre-build the audio** as one playlist in scene order (monitor beeps, chanting, the climax track) so nobody breaks character fumbling a phone.
- **Aftercare for four**, not two. Blankets, water, sugar, and a proper debrief a few days later since it's a first scene with this couple.

## "The demon is leaving" (Act 3)

Best version stacks several at once, all cueing off each other:

1. **Monitor goes wild** during the strapon climax, alarms screaming, then one held beat of total silence.
2. **Aimee's performance**: builds to convulsing and screaming, then sudden, complete limpness. Coach this beforehand so she knows the shape.
3. **All sound cuts at once** except a slow, calm, steady heartbeat. The demon audio (a pitch-dropping growl fading to nothing) dies with it.
4. **Break the sigil**: the wax star scraped or cracked off her back by the priest. Good sensation, perfect symbolism.
5. **The cleansing**: cum lube injection as the final rite (needle-free lube launcher), priest declares her clean.
6. **Cold air** from a fan across her body as "it leaves", plus smoke machine if the dungeon has one.
7. **The reveal**: hood and blindfold come off only now. First thing she sees is candlelight and three calm faces. Reborn. Straight into aftercare from that beat.

## Safety notes (the boring vital bit)

- **Hood + mask + restraints = she can't speak clearly.** Non-verbal safeword is mandatory: a bell or ball in hand to drop, plus scheduled hand-squeeze check-ins from Rob. Raven needs a signal too for the impact.
- **The ventilator mask must be theatrical only.** No actual airflow restriction, and it must come off in one second.
- **The injection**: a capped or blunt needle pressed to the skin after the alcohol swab sells it completely, since she can't see. If a real needle goes in, it's sterile, single-use, gloved hands, sharps tin. No reuse, ever.
- **Wax**: low-melt-point play candles or soy, tested on Rob's own arm first, height controls temperature. Back is a good canvas, keep off the spine's bony ridge.
- **Strapon hygiene**: fresh or barrier-covered toy, and the lube launcher is single-user.
- **First scene with new partners: build in an off-ramp.** Agree beforehand what "we skip to the ending" looks like if anyone's not feeling it, so the scene can land softly instead of crashing.
