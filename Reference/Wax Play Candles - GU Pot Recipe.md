# Wax Play Candles — GU Pot Recipe

*Captured 2026-09-04. Rob's idea: upcycle GU dessert pots (glass ramekins) into wax play candles.*

- **Pot size**: standard round GU ramekin, ~6.5cm across, ~4cm deep, ~130ml to the brim. Oven-safe glass, handles pour/burn heat fine.
- **Wax per pot**: fill to 1cm below rim (~95-100ml) → **85-100g of wax per pot**. A 1kg bag does ~10 pots with wicks and spillage.
- **Melt ratio**: flakes settle, so melt roughly **2x the pot's volume in flake form** to get one full pot.
- **Wax type**: **soy or plain paraffin only**. Soy melts ~45-50°C, the skin-safe sweet spot. Skip beeswax and anything with stearin (both run hotter), skip fragrance oils (irritate skin at play temps). Dye chips are fine.
- **Wick**: **one wick, sized up** — Eco 8/10 or CL10-ish for soy in a 6.5cm pot (one size above what a scented-candle maker would use), for a full edge-to-edge melt pool to pour from. Multi-wick only earns its keep at 8cm+ pots; 3 wicks in a 6.5cm pot would crowd, overheat, and drain the pot fast. If one wick isn't pooling to the edges, size up the wick next batch rather than adding wicks.
- **Burn technique**: light 20-30 min before pouring to develop the pool. Rim gets warm after ~20 min lit. Single centred wick lets him pour off one side without dunking the flame.
- **Sources**: [Home Made Productions GU pot candle tutorial](https://homemadeproductions.co.uk/2020/05/how-to-make-upcycled-gu-pot-candles/), [GU's own reuse page](https://gudesserts.com/pages/reuse).
